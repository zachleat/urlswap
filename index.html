URLswap

One "set" of interchangeable domains per line, comma-separated (e.g. localhost:8080, dev.example.com, example.com). Clicking the bookmarklet on a URL matching a domain in a set jumps to the next one in that set, keeping the path/query/hash. Entries without a scheme default to http:// for localhost/127.0.0.1 and https:// for everything else; write the scheme explicitly to override.
